The most notable thing about the 2025 national sniper competition was that members of our Federation were among the top places.

Second place went to Armen Babayan, an economist by profession and director of the Grand Candy company. As he tells it himself, he has been shooting since childhood, from a slingshot to a Victrix and a Sako. Years of shooting experience and hard work let him lead throughout the season.

In congratulating our teammate, we pass on his own words: put your trust in your weapon, your readiness and your preparation. Peace will be kept only that way.
