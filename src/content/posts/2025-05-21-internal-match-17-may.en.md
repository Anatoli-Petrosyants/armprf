The Armenian Precision Rifle Federation's internal match was shot on 17 May 2025.

It ran in a friendly, warm atmosphere. The winners received prizes provided by the match sponsors, BallistiQ and Zinvir.

Athletes from the Georgian precision rifle federation were guests at the match as well.

The Federation regards matches as central to developing the sport, and is committed to making them a regular fixture.
