The winter rimfire match was shot in Yerevan on 8 March, from 25 to 200 metres. Discipline: .22LR rimfire PRS, in the Rimfire and Junior divisions. Match director: Ani Melkonyan.

## Why it mattered

This was the first match where more than half the field was new to the federation. Four loaner rifles and 1500 rounds were provided, so that not owning a rifle was no reason to stay home.

Six shooters competed in the junior class.

## What the match showed

At short range .22LR forgives nothing: at 200 metres the bullet drifts about as much as centerfire does at 800. That is exactly what makes it the fastest teacher.

Results were recorded by the range officer and sent to every competitor.
