During the Russia–Ukraine war one of the best ballistic calculators for iOS, **Strelok Pro**, went away.

Armen, who fought in the Artsakh war, has built an Armenian one together with his friends: **BALLISTiQ — Shooter's Assistant**.

Download it, use it, tell them what is missing — let it get better.

Well done, lads. This is a serious step for the development of gun culture in Armenia.

[Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ballistiq-shooters-assistant/id6476917854)
