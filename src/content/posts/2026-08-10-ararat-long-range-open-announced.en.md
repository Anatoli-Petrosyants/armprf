On 20–21 September, Ararat Province hosts the longest match of the season: the **Ararat Long Range Open**. Two days, 12 stages, and a maximum distance of 1100 metres.

## The essentials

- **Discipline** — centerfire long range
- **Divisions** — Open, Hunter, Production
- **Distances** — 400–1100 metres
- **Round count** — 140
- **Entry** — 15,000 AMD
- **Match director** — Tigran Sargsyan
- **Where** — Ararat Province, TODO(content) range

## Schedule

- **Saturday 20.09** — registration 07:30, safety briefing 08:15, stages 1–6.
- **Sunday 21.09** — stages 7–12, awards around 16:00.

## What to bring

140 rounds, a verified zero, and clothing that works in wind. Camping on site with your own tent is possible.

Registration closes on 13 September, or when the field reaches 40 shooters. Write to us to enter.
