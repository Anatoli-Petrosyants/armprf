On 30 October, Arordi LLC and the Armenian Precision Rifle Federation (ARMPRF) signed a memorandum of cooperation aimed at developing the sport of precision rifle shooting in Armenia.

Under the partnership, the two sides will share professional experience and knowledge, and Arordi will support athletes in acquiring and importing specialised rifles and related equipment.

For us this is more than a new partnership: it is a chance to contribute to building precision shooting in Armenia.
