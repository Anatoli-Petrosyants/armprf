Meet our champion.

A programmer by profession, Aro Periyan bought his first rifle seven months ago, a Victrix Venus in 6.5 Creedmoor, and became one of Azatazen's regulars. Training consistently with the Federation, he was competing with experienced, skilled shooters in a short space of time.

And it is no joke: five hundred rounds in three days before the match.

Congratulations.
