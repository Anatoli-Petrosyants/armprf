## Who can take part

The match is open to every adult citizen of the Republic of Armenia. You will need:

- **a rifle** chambered from .223 Rem up to and including .338 Lapua Mag,
- **a scope** mounted on it,
- **to register** through the form below,
- **the entry fee** of 10,000 AMD.
