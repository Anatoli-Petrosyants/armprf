For the first time, we shoot the **500 Yard Ethical Hunter Challenge**: one cold bore shot at a 5 inch (13 cm) target, 500 yards out, with up to two follow-ups if the first one misses.

The video opens on the range with an introduction to the challenge, then goes prone behind the rifle for the shots at an animal silhouette.

Come and take part, and let's become better marksmen together. If you want to join, leave a comment under the video and we will get in touch.

The rules and the results so far are on the [challenge page](/leaderboards/ethical-hunter-500/).
