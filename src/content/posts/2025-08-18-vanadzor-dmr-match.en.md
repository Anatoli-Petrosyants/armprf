On Saturday the Armenian Precision Rifle Federation held its match for self-loading rifles: DMR (Designated Marksman Rifle), the SVD and comparable rifles.

The match was organised at Azatazen's Vanadzor range. Experienced shooters and newcomers both took part, and most importantly of all, women took an active part as well.

For Armenia's survival and security it is vital to preserve and improve the citizen's right to be armed.

Join us, arm yourselves, train.
