This weekend the Federation held another of its internal matches, put on for shooters who enjoy working with .22LR calibre rifles.

The match brought experienced shooters and newcomers together, with a sporting spirit, real competition and a warm atmosphere.

Thank you to everyone who took part and everyone who supported it. Our aim is to develop precision rifle shooting in Armenia and to give everyone the chance to sharpen their own skills.
