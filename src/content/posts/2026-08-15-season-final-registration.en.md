The 2026 season final will be shot on **18 October in Gyumri**. Registration is open and closes on 11 October, or when the field reaches 40.

## The essentials

- Eight stages from 100 to 800 metres, Open and Hunter divisions.
- The rimfire division runs its own 25–200 m bay on the same day.
- Round count: 90 centerfire, or 60 rimfire.
- Entry: 10,000 AMD, or 7,000 AMD for members.

## Why it decides the season

The standings count your best four matches. Right now 11 points separate the top three, which means the final is decisive for all of them.

## How to enter

Fill in the registration form on the event page. Payment is taken on site at check-in. If you have to cancel, tell us at least 48 hours ahead so the waiting list can move.
