After three months of work the core safety structures at the range are in place. This is a short, honest update, with nothing dressed up.

## Done

- **Side berms:** both standing, 4 metres high.
- **Backstop:** reinforced and proofed with fire from 900 metres.
- **Access track:** passable by a normal car in dry weather.
- **Firing line levelled:** 12 positions, marked out.

## Left

- Permanent target frames from 300 to 1200 metres (planned for July 2026, moved to autumn).
- Shelter over the firing line.
- Water supply and toilets.
- Signage and safety marking.

## Where we need help

We need AR500 steel plate, welding work for target stands, and transport for heavy material. If you can help with any of it, write to us.

The next update comes in October, after the season final.
