The last match of the 2025 season was shot on 2 November in Ararat Province, with 28 competitors. Ten stages, 300 to 900 metres, 110 rounds. Match director: Gor Petrosyan.

## 900 metres, for the first time

This match put a 900 m stage on the board for the first time. The hit rate there was 34%, which was about what we expected. For most of the field it was the longest shot they had ever taken under a clock.

## Two points

The season title was decided on the final stage, by two points. A whole season, ten days of shooting, came down to one shot.

The full season standings were sent to every competitor.
