The season standings aggregate every official match of the year. They show who is consistent, not who got lucky once.

## Scoring

- At each match a shooter earns a **percentage of stage points**, measured against the match winner's total.
- That percentage converts to **up to 100 points** for the match.
- The **best four matches** of the season count. A fifth and beyond are dropped.
- Missing a match scores 0 — there is no penalty beyond that.

## Divisions

Standings are kept separately for Open, Hunter and Rimfire. Use the filter to pick a division.

## Ties

On equal totals, the shooter with the higher single best result goes ahead. If those are equal too, both share the rank.
