The rimfire ladder is the federation's entry challenge. You can run it with any .22LR rifle and 50 rounds.

## Format

- **10 targets** at 25, 50, 75, 100, 125, 150, 165, 180, 190 and 200 metres.
- **5 shots** per target, 50 in total.
- Every hit scores **1 point**. Maximum **50 points**.
- Total time: **12 minutes**.
- Position is free, but no bench and no table.

## Rules

1. Any .22LR rifle, any ammunition.
2. Zeroing is allowed before the ladder starts, not during it.
3. Targets are painted before every attempt.
4. Attempts are unlimited; your best score stays on the board.

## Why start here

Fifty rounds of .22LR cost about the same as one round of 6.5 Creedmoor. The rate of learning is the same. The price is not.
