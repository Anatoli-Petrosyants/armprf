The longest match of the season. Two days, 12 stages, and a maximum distance of 1100 metres.

## Schedule

- **Saturday 20.09** — registration 07:30, safety briefing 08:15, stages 1–6.
- **Sunday 21.09** — stages 7–12, awards around 16:00.

## What to bring

140 rounds, a verified zero, and clothing that works in wind. Camping on site with your own tent is possible.

Registration closes on 13 September, or when the field reaches 40 shooters.
